# Urgency & Scarcity Patterns

How Nick creates urgency and scarcity in his CTAs.

## Calendar Slot Scarcity (most common — 10+ videos)
> "Right now, we have a handful of spots open."

> "Currently, we're all booked up other than three slots here on Tuesday. So, if you guys want to grab a 1 p.m., 2 p.m., or 5:00 p.m. Once these slots are filled up, we're not accepting any more calls for a few days."

> "If you click the link in the description below and you don't see any availability on the calendar, then just check back in 24 to 48 hours and there should be some slots open up."

> "These slots fill up fast every single time I drop a video. So, don't wait to do this tomorrow because if you wait to do this tomorrow, it's most likely that the slots are going to be filled up for days or even weeks because of how many people watch these videos."

## Monthly Capacity Limits (5+ videos)
> "We're capped at how many serious investors we can help on a one-on-one basis."

> "We only take on a limited number of new clients to ensure our one-on-one quality."

> "We don't accept everybody. We're only accepting about 30 people this month."

> "We only have room for a few dedicated professionals to maintain the level of elite quality that we provide."

## Window Closing / Deadline (conspiracy/reset videos — 5+)
> "The window to position yourself is open, but it won't stay that way forever."

> "Don't wait for the distraction cycle or headlines to pull you away."

> "You need to move with a sense of urgency as if something big is coming 24 hours from right now. That's how I operate every single day. And that's how I became a millionaire before 30."

> "When you click that link, if the calendar is full, it simply means our one-on-one capacity is at its limit for now. We only open new spots once a current partner is fully positioned."

## Market Timing Urgency (bear market videos — 5+)
> "By the time later comes, you've already missed the boat."

> "By the time virtually all the banks pass it, it's going to be too late. You want to get the jump on the gun."

> "Don't wait for the exchanges to hit zero."

> "Educate yourself now. Don't wait for it to be too late because you're just thinking to yourself, 'Ah, well, I'll figure it out later.'"

## Training Removal Threat (2 videos)
> "This training, I'm probably not going to leave it up forever. We're most likely going to transition over to a free webinar."

## Time-Limited Offers (rare — 1 video)
> "We just opened the doors for a 7-day free trial of Altcoin Pro and it's going to end January 1st."

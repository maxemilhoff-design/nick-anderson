# Call to Action Index

Nick uses a **two-stage CTA architecture** across virtually every video: a mid-video soft CTA and an end-video hard CTA. The CTAs follow consistent patterns but vary in framing based on the content type.

## Active Main Offers (current)

### 1. Altcoin Pro Accelerator (High-Ticket Coaching) — MAIN OFFER
- **Appears in:** ~30 of 35 videos
- **Price:** Not stated on video (strategy call required)
- **Minimum:** $50,000 in crypto (stated in ~15 videos)
- **Duration:** 5 months, one-on-one
- **Includes:** Bi-weekly 1:1 coaching, weekly group calls, Slack access, research team, project vetting
- **See:** [[cta-accelerator-pitch|Accelerator Pitch Variations]]

### 2. iMerchant Solutions (Affiliate — Payment Processing) — MAIN OFFER
- **Target:** Business owners, 1099 contractors, agency owners, shop owners
- **Link:** `imerchantsolutions.com/apply/AGT-00048`
- **What it does:** Payment processing diagnostic — reduces fees, lifts authorization rates, crypto acceptance, next-day payouts, 74-country orchestration
- **Best content fit:** Bank/Visa/Mastercard exposé videos, Great Reset, YieldGate, business-angle content
- **Two-offer stack:** iMerchant fixes business revenue leaks → Altcoin Pro teaches what to do with the profit
- **See:** [[cta-imerchant|iMerchant Full Details + Verbatim CTA Copy]]

## CTA Framing Patterns

### By Content Type
- **Conspiracy/Reset videos** → "Digital dome" / "gateway closing" / "positioned before the door locks" → **Altcoin Pro**
- **Bear market TA videos** → "Earn passive income while your portfolio bleeds" / "make your crypto pay rent" → **Altcoin Pro**
- **News/institutional videos** → "Retail chases price predictions, institutions position in infrastructure" → **Altcoin Pro**
- **Bank/Visa/Mastercard exposé videos** → "They're running the same game on your business revenue" → **iMerchant** then bridge to **Altcoin Pro**
- **Educational videos** → "You've learned the blueprint. Now you need the plan" → **Altcoin Pro**
- **VSL/Sales videos** → Full problem/agitate/solve/testimonials/offer/guarantee arc → **Altcoin Pro University**

### The Two-Offer Stack (new pattern)
When a video covers banks/payment rails/financial system content, Nick can run both offers:
1. **Mid-video:** iMerchant — "Fix the leaks in your business revenue"
2. **Bridge:** "Now, once you've fixed the leaks, what are you doing with the profit?"
3. **End-video:** Altcoin Pro — "Learn to make that profit work for you"

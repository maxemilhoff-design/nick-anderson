# Accelerator Pitch Variations

The Altcoin Pro Accelerator pitch appears in ~30 of 35 videos. Below are the distinct variations, organized by framing angle.

## Variation 1: The Infrastructure Survival Pitch (conspiracy/reset videos)

**Bridge:** "Simply knowing that a reset is coming is not enough. You need the blueprint to operate during the Great Reset."

**Pitch:**
> "And right now, while most people are watching their portfolios bleed out during the bare market, the investors inside of the Altcoin Pro Accelerator program are earning passive yield on their XRP and their other idol altcoins every single month without losing sleep to the market volatility. And every single week, what we do is we jump on live calls where we break down exactly what we're looking at in the market, what we're buying, what we're selling, entries, exits, and we do live Q&A so you can get your questions answered in real time."

**Used in:** [[nesara-titanic]], [[1992-movie-589]], [[simpsons-antikythera]]

And that's exactly what's inside of the Altcoin Pro Accelerator program. You're shown how the institutions are building their portfolios. You'll learn which coins are positioned for institutional adoption based on real on-chain liquidity flows. You'll learn how smart money is earning passive yield on their idle holdings without needing to sell."

## The Funnel Structure (consistent across all variations)

1. **Free training video** (link in description) → viewer watches
2. **Apply Today button** → viewer fills out qualification form
3. **Strategy call** with Nick or a coach → qualification + pitch
4. **Program enrollment** → 5 months, one-on-one

**The form asks:**
> "How long have you been in crypto? How long have you been following our channel? What's your day-to-day life look like? What's your current portfolio value? How much disposable capital do you have to invest?"

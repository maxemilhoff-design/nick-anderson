# Sign-off Patterns

Nick's video closings are ritualistic — the same phrases in the same order, building familiarity and community identity.

## The Standard Sign-off (appears in 30+ of 35 videos)
> "As always, you know what to do. Stay bullish."

Often followed by:
> "Hey, hey, hey."

## The Full Closing Sequence (most complete version)
> "So, if you get value out of videos like this, make sure you subscribe to the channel. Make sure you like it, share with a friend that you think would go down the rabbit hole, and leave a comment below. And as always, I will see you at the top. You know what to do. Stay bullish. Hey, hey, hey."

## Variations

### The "See You" Stack
> "I will see you on the next video. I will see you through the link in the description below. As always, you know what to do. Stay bullish."

> "I will see you at the top. I will see you on the call when you book a meeting below. As always, you know what to do. Stay bullish. Hey, hey, hey."

### The "Bags" Metaphor Close
> "We're backing up our trucks all the way to the bank, grabbing the bags, packing them, and stacking them, leaving no bags left behind."

### The Dollar Debasement Close
> "As always, the spending power of the dollar is going down in value. That's a fact based on inflation. Blockchain technology, distributed ledger technology, and yield generation strategies are becoming more popular and integrated into the global financial system. That's the truth."

### The "Running to the Top" Community Close
> "And together, we're all running to the top."

### The Rabbit Hole Tease
> "Share with a friend that you think would go down the rabbit hole."
> "I'm going to go even further down the rabbit hole in my next video."

### The AI Video Disclaimer (newer videos — 3+)
> "As always, these videos are purely for educational and entertainment purposes only, not financial advice. So, make sure to do your own research and never invest more than you can afford to lose."

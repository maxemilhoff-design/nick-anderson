# Social Proof & Testimonials

Nick uses social proof sparingly in most videos but heavily in the VSL. Here's every instance extracted across 35 videos.

## Named Student Results (from VSL — [[project-deepfake]])
- **Eric** — "a crypto newbie who turned $85,000 into over $350,000 with what he learned"
- **Aid** — "paid off a chunk of his mortgage with $62,000 in portfolio profits"
- **Yoni** — "erased $260,000 in medical school debt, now sharing his top DeFi strategies as part of our research team"
- **Dylan** — "got a $20,000 airdrop and was able to book a trip to Italy"
- **Julian** — "made over $23,000 in one week after applying what he learned from our technical analysis training"
- **Neil** — "started with only $6,000 and grew his portfolio to over $100,000"

## Aggregate Social Proof
> "over a thousand investors" / "over a thousand of our students" (VSL)
> "hundreds of other members, over 100 plus expert curated classes" (free trial video)
> "about 50 to 60 of you guys fly out from around the world to learn from our team in person" (Vegas event)

## Team Credential Proof (used instead of testimonials in 5+ videos)

> "My partner Ryan Horse... had one of the first web3 education companies in the world."

## Personal Credential Proof
> "I became a millionaire before 30."
> "For over 8 years, my team and I have been creating daily content to educate traders worldwide."
> "founder of Bull Runners with over 350,000 subscribers on YouTube and a daily newsletter reaching nearly 50,000 investors"
> "I've been in crypto since 2016."

## Implicit Social Proof (referencing program results without names)
> "The investors inside of our program are earning passive yield on their XRP every single day."
> "The core members that joined our accelerator program back in August of 2025... were able to take profit on XRP... securing their capital, and they've been earning yield on it ever since while the vast majority of the general public is down over 60%."
> "I know this because I have students earning that exactly right now inside of our altcoin pro accelerator program."

## Testimonial CTA (directing viewers to read testimonials)
> "I don't want you to just take my word for it. Listen to some of the case studies through the link in the description below where you can hear from our clients and then just scroll through these testimonials."
> "You can listen to some of the results from our clients over the years."

## Key Observation
Nick relies more on **team credentials and institutional framing** than on classic testimonials in regular content videos. The heavy named-testimonials approach is reserved for the VSL/sales letter format. In standard videos, social proof is usually implicit ("our investors are earning yield right now") rather than specific.

The mechanism by which ordinary investors can "become their own bank." DeFi liquidity pools and yield protocols offer 10-200%+ APY on crypto holdings.

## Nick's Framing
This is what banks fear most. The [[the-clarity-act|The Clarity Act]] battle is fundamentally about whether banks can prevent people from earning yield on their own crypto. Banks earn yield on your deposits (paying you 0.01% APY); DeFi lets you keep that yield yourself.

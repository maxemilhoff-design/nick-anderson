# Invisible Plumbing

Nick's metaphor for how institutional infrastructure gets built — quietly, in regulatory working groups, behind closed doors, before anyone knows it's happening.

The insight: institutions don't hype adoption; they deploy it silently.

**Key phrase:** "It's not speculation, it's infrastructure."

Nick uses this to explain why XRP's price doesn't reflect its institutional adoption — the plumbing is being installed before the water gets turned on.

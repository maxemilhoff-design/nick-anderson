## Nick's TA Toolkit
1. **RSI + Stochastic RSI + MACD** — "confluence" of all three confirming = signal
2. **Bitcoin Stock-to-Flow Rainbow** — color-coded halving cycle indicator
3. **Tether Dominance** — money fleeing/entering altcoins

## Key Principles
- "The trend is your friend"
- "Break of structure" = the only confirmation of trend reversal (clearing previous swing high)
- "Regular bullish divergence" = price makes lower low, RSI makes higher low
- "History has a funny way of repeating itself every single cycle"
- Law of diminishing returns across cycles

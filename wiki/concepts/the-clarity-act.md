The crypto market structure legislation moving through the US Senate. Already passed House 294-134 in July 2025. Would classify XRP as a digital commodity under federal law.

## Nick's Framing
The "federal green light" for institutional money. Banks are using it as leverage to extend the yield ban from the [[the-genius-act|The Genius Act]] to all exchanges and DeFi platforms. The bill that either opens the floodgates or keeps them locked.

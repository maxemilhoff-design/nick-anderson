The process of putting real-world financial instruments (stocks, bonds, real estate, treasuries) onto blockchain rails as digital tokens.

## Nick's Framing
[[blackrock|BlackRock]] and institutional players are moving ALL global securities onto blockchain. [[blackrock|Larry Fink]] has stated this publicly. Valued at $100-300 trillion. This is the largest wealth transfer mechanism in human history.

## Key Evidence
- [[dtcc|DTCC]] processes $2.5 quadrillion annually, aspires to tokenize all $100T in assets
- [[blackrock|BlackRock]]'s BUIDL tokenized money market fund (multi-chain)
- [[tokenization|Dubai Land Department]] completed Phase 2 (7.8M in property tokenized)
- [[tokenization|Amazon]] approved as vendor on XRP ledger

Nick's concept that portfolio size determines not just available strategies but emotional experience and decision-making quality.

## Nick's Framing
The difference between winners and losers isn't intelligence, information, or timing — it's capital size. Different capital levels ($10K, $25K, $50K, $100K+) require fundamentally different strategies. This is why the $50K minimum exists for [[altcoin-pro|Altcoin Pro Accelerator]].

## Key Quote
"Most people in crypto are losing money and they have no idea why. They did the research, bought quality projects, held through dips, and still ended up worse off. Someone bought the exact same coin at the exact same time and made money. The difference is capital size."

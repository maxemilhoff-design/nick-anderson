# Miner Capitulation

When Bitcoin price falls, inefficient miners are forced off the network and sell reserve BTC, accelerating the downturn.

## Nick's Indicator
The "blocks since difficulty bottom" chart historically shows the bear market low occurring during the transition from dark red → blue → green.

Part of Nick's [[bear-market-cycles|confluence-based TA]] approach — never relied on alone, always confirmed with RSI, Stochastic RSI, and [[tether-dominance|Tether Dominance]].

# Tether Market Cap Dominance

Using Tether's market cap as a percentage of total altcoin market cap to confirm bull/bear phases.

## Nick's Framework
- Tether dominance rises → money fleeing altcoins (bearish)
- Tether dominance peaks and rolls over → altcoin recovery begins (bullish)
- A "break of structure" on Tether dominance to the upside = bear market confirmed

Used as a leading/lagging indicator paired against Bitcoin and Total 2.

The new global financial messaging standard that replaced SWIFT's MT format. Nick calls it "the new language banks speak."

## Key Dates
- **July 14, 2025**: Fedwire migration complete
- **November 22, 2025**: SWIFT migration complete  
- **April 2026**: Bank of England mandatory deadline for all institutions

## Nick's Framing
The "rails" for programmable money and tokenized assets. Certain cryptocurrencies are "ISO 20022-aligned" — XRP, XLM, HBAR, XDC, Algorand. This standard is what makes [[the-great-reset|The Great Reset]] technically possible.

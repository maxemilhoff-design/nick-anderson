# The Genius Act

The first US federal framework for stablecoins, signed by [[donald-trump|Trump]] in July 2025.

Nick frames it as a "win for crypto" with a compromise: stablecoin issuers cannot pay yield. Banks agreed to this initially, then came back trying to extend the ban to all exchanges and DeFi platforms via [[the-clarity-act|The Clarity Act]].

The betrayal that started the "war" between Trump and the banks.

# Evidence Layering (The Proof Stack)

Nick layers 4 types of evidence in a specific progression within every video:

## The Stack (in order)

### Tier 1 — Official/Documented
Federal Reserve press releases, SWIFT announcements, SEC filings, Congressional vote counts, Bank of England road maps. Establishes credibility with real institutional data first.

**Phrases:** "This is all documented." / "You can read this yourself."

### Tier 2 — Corporate/Executive Statements
Larry Fink quotes, Brad Garlinghouse statements, Trump speeches. Names CEOs and references their public positions. Often embedded as video clips.

**Phrases:** "Listen to what [person] said..."

### Tier 3 — Pattern/Coincidence Stacking
Multiple "coincidences" listed in rapid succession (the "receipt stack"). The more items, the harder to dismiss. The numbered list in the Time Travel Conspiracy video (17 items) is the extreme example.

**Phrases:** "What are the odds?" / "You cannot make this up."

### Tier 4 — Esoteric/Numerological
[[gematria|Gematria]], Sneakers movie, [[bearable-guy|Bearable Guy Riddles]], Ingersol Lockwood books. Introduced LAST, after institutional evidence. Always hedged ("whether you believe this or not") but treated as confirmatory.

## The Effect
Builds a credible institutional case first, then uses conspiratorial material to give the pattern a mythological dimension — makes it feel predestined and irresistible.

## Key Principle
Nick NEVER leads with conspiracy. He earns credibility with Tier 1-2, then expands into Tier 3-4. This is why his audience trusts the esoteric content — it comes after the facts.

# Hook Architecture

Nick uses a single consistent opening architecture across virtually all videos:

## The Formula

1. **Pattern-interrupt premise** — A bold "What if..." or "While you were watching X, Y was happening" or a shocking institutional factoid
2. **Chain of evidence** — 2-4 rapid-fire connected events linking something historical, something current, and XRP
3. **Stakes declaration** — Names the scale ("$300 trillion," "the entire global financial system," "the biggest wealth transfer in human history")
4. **CTA trigger** — "Comment 777 if you're feeling blessed, if you're feeling bullish"
5. **Subscribe line** — "If you're going to become the first millionaire in your family tree, confirm it by tapping the subscribe button"
6. **Launch phrase** — "Let's run it"
7. **Channel welcome** — "All right, bull runners. Welcome back to the channel"

## Hook Types Nick Uses
- **Curiosity-gap**: "What if the most famous shipwreck in history wasn't an accident..."
- **Bold-claim**: "$300 trillion onto blockchain technology"
- **Pattern-interrupt**: "While you've been watching wars in the Middle East..." / Opens with celebrity death
- **Proof**: "Mastercard just doubled down on Ripple, publishing a major post..."
- **Controversy**: Banks vs. crypto, Trump vs. banks
- **Urgency**: "XRP could drop another 30-50% from these levels"

## Key Insight
The bigger and more conspiratorial the video, the more historical the opening anchor (Titanic 1912, Ingersol Lockwood 1889, Project Looking Glass 1980s). News-driven videos open with the institutional factoid. The hook ALWAYS connects something tangible to XRP.

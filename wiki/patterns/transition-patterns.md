# Transition Patterns

How Nick moves between sections within videos.

## Escalation Transitions
- **"Now, here's where it gets [really interesting / absolutely insane / even wilder]"** — Primes viewer before each new reveal
- **"And this is where things get really strange..."** — Signals conspiracy escalation
- **"Buckle up"** — Big claim incoming

## Confirmation Transitions  
- **"Got it?"** — Rhetorical check that the pattern landed
- **"Now, I know what you're thinking..."** — Preemptively answers skeptic objection

## Contrast Transitions
- **"While retail [X], institutions [Y]"** — Core framework for positioning viewer
- **"But here's the thing..."** — Reframes a setback as actually bullish

## Structural Transitions
- **"Let me show you..." / "Let me break it down..."** — Narrative → explanation
- **"And here's the timing..."** — Connects two separate events into coordinated pattern
- **"Now, speaking of DeFi..."** — Bridge to CTA
- **"But let's get back into the content"** — Returns from CTA to substance
- **"Listen to what [person] said..."** — Introduces a clip

## Numbered Sections
Uses explicit numbered points in analytical sections: "Point number one... Point number two..." Especially in TA/chart videos.

## Between News and Charts
Hard pivot: "From a fundamental perspective... now let me show you the chart" or "So what does this mean for XRP?"

## Causal Chains
Heavy use of "because" as rhetorical connector to build urgency through cascading logic.

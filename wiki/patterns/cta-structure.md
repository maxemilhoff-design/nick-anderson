# CTA Structure (Two-Stage Architecture)

Nick uses a consistent two-stage CTA in every standard video.

## Stage 1 — Mid-Video Soft CTA (Sell the Program)

**Structure:**
1. Problem: "If you're navigating this alone, you're going to get left behind"
2. Contrast: Banks pay 0.01% APY vs. DeFi yields 20-200%
3. Qualification: "If you have at least $50,000 in crypto..."
4. Offer: "Watch the free training video in the first link below, then book a strategy call"
5. Objection handle: "Even if our coaching program is not the right thing for you, worst case you'll walk away with more information"

**Program:** [[altcoin-pro|Altcoin Pro Accelerator]] / [[sgs-system|SGS System]]

**Tagline (verbatim):** "Learn how the top 1% of investors secure, grow, and scale their portfolios without losing sleep over scams, hacks, and volatility."

## Stage 2 — End-of-Video Hard CTA

**Structure:**
1. Restate urgency: "slots fill up fast every time I drop a video"
2. Social proof: Student results by first name + dollar amount
3. Subscribe: "Make sure you subscribe"
4. Like: "Make sure you like it"
5. Share: "Share with a friend who would go down the rabbit hole"
6. Cross-promote: Tease related video
7. Sign-off: "As always, you know what to do. Stay bullish. Hey, hey, hey."

## Exception
The VSL/sales video (project-deepfake / ts_fVNI-6iw) follows a classic problem/agitate/solve/testimonials/offer/guarantee arc with no "comment 777" or "bull runners welcome back." This is a different content type.

# Artlist AI Prompt Pack — Nick Anderson

Copy-paste prompts for **Artlist's AI video + image generators** (and usable as stock-search keywords), organized by Nick's content pillars. Built to match his visual tone: dramatic, high-contrast, cinematic, faintly ominous — the "hidden truth about to break" feeling that pairs with his curiosity-gap hooks.

## How to use
1. Pick the pillar that matches the beat you're scripting (see [[content-formula]]).
2. Paste a prompt into Artlist's AI video generator. Generate 3–4 variations, keep the best.
3. For stock search instead of AI: strip the camera/style tail and search the bolded **subject** keywords.
4. Cut clips 2–4 seconds each under Nick's VO — B-roll should escalate as the evidence stack builds ([[evidence-layering]]).

## House style (append to any prompt for consistency)
> `cinematic, high dynamic range, dramatic volumetric lighting, deep shadows, teal-and-gold color grade, subtle film grain, shallow depth of field, slow deliberate camera move, 24fps, photorealistic, no text, no logos, no watermark`

Nick's palette: **gold/amber** (money, XRP, "the new system") vs **cold blue/teal** (banks, surveillance, the old system). Lean gold for opportunity beats, cold blue for threat/control beats.

## Prompt-writing recipe (to make your own)
`[subject + specific detail] + [setting] + [action/motion] + [camera move] + [lighting] + [house style tail]`

Keep it to one clear focal subject per clip. Match the color to the beat (gold = opportunity, cold blue = threat). Escalate visual intensity as the evidence stack builds toward the CTA.

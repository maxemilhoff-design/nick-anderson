> Make this exact image but in a lot higher quality so it's 4K and looks like a professional photographer took it — razor-sharp detail, crisp skin texture, clean lighting, no blur.
>

>
> Then move the praying man (Elon Musk) to the right side and make him stand calm and powerful, half in shadow, with faint gold puppet-strings coming down from his hand.
>
> Then replace the space scene and the slot-machine payment terminal with a dark glowing chessboard where the big gold XRP coin and a big white "X" coin are the two king pieces, and a small Bitcoin coin is knocked over on its side.
>
> Then replace the purple rocket area with a Cross River Bank building in the back under a dark storm sky with lightning.
>
> Then change the big text from "1999 XRP = X-MONEY!" to "IT WAS ALL PLANNED!" in the same bold chunky 3D style, white and gold with a thick outline and a glowing red accent.
>
> Keep the fire in the bottom corners and the dramatic lighting. Don't make any other changes, and make sure the quality is high in 4K with really good lighting and sharp detail.

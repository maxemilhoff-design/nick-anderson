# Artlist Shot List — "Elon / June 2026 Cluster"

Scene-by-scene AI-video B-roll prompts keyed to the actual VO of this script. Each block = one beat. Generate 3–4 variations per prompt in Artlist, keep the best, cut 2–4s each under the narration. Intensity escalates as the evidence stack builds ([[evidence-layering]]).

## ⚠️ Read first — real people & logos
Artlist's AI generator will NOT reliably (or legally) render **Elon Musk, Trump, Sacks, Thiel, Saylor, Dalio** or real logos (SpaceX, Mastercard, Ripple, PayPal, Palantir, Nasdaq). For those exact moments use **licensed editorial/stock footage, official clips, or your own screen-recordings** (tweets, filings, charts). Use the AI prompts below for the **atmospheric / symbolic / conceptual** B-roll between those beats — that's where AI shines and where you fill most of the runtime.

## House style tail (append to every prompt)
> `cinematic, high dynamic range, dramatic volumetric lighting, deep shadows, teal-and-gold color grade, subtle film grain, shallow depth of field, slow deliberate camera move, 24fps, photorealistic, no text, no logos, no watermark`

Palette logic: **gold/amber** = opportunity, XRP, the new system · **cold blue/teal** = threat, banks, surveillance, the old system.
